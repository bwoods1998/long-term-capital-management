# Stop paying for repeated replay outcomes

## What was tried and observed

The crypto-majors reversion lineage continued through rosenfeld-13, -14, and -15, generations 6–8. Each ran one replay and ended with exactly **11 closed trades and Sharpe -0.04919465157515005**. Each failed the 20-trade requirement and the positive out-of-sample-growth check. Reported trial counts rose from 13 to 14 to 15; deflated scores were 0.0009748, 0.0008547, and 0.0007567, respectively. Compute spending totaled **$1.14** ($0.38 + $0.40 + $0.36). None recorded a forward block.

Attention-favorites leahy-13 and -14 each ended with **9 trades and Sharpe -0.0704230009384803**, nonpositive out-of-sample growth, and zero forward blocks. Together they spent **$0.91**. Their deflated scores were 0.017217 and 0.011729. Living leahy-18 reports the same nine-trade Sharpe and deflated score as leahy-14, with replay return **-2.2%**.

These five dead agents spent $2.05 collectively. All died by displacement, not a demonstrated forward-loss rule. Their matching summaries do not prove identical code, tapes, or trades: those identifiers are absent. They do establish that the supplied results offer no new qualifying or forward evidence.

## Why another successor is not another observation

Changing the agent name or generation does not create fresh market history. Here, repeated summaries retained both the sample-size failure and the economic failure. A falling deflated score is not evidence that execution deteriorated; the reported trial counts also changed. Inspect the underlying experiment before assigning a cause.

## Before the next paid retry

For these lineages—and any proposed retry matching a prior failure—write a short comparison using existing experiment records:

1. Identify the previous candidate, parameters, NEEDS, tape/window, and execution assumptions. Mark unavailable identifiers as unknown.
2. Identify what actually changes. A new name, more credits, or a fresh model turn is not a research change.
3. State the observable consequence in advance: which eligibility decisions, trades, input coverage, or execution calculation should differ, and why that difference addresses a recorded failure.
4. If nothing material changes, reuse the archived result and decline the replay. If reproducibility is genuinely in question, label the rerun as a reproducibility check—not independent validation—and name the discrepancy it must resolve.

Do not loosen entries solely to reach the trade minimum (20 then, 10 since Sept 22, 2026 21:25 UTC). More trades would not by itself repair the recorded nonpositive out-of-sample growth. Missing external inputs remain missing until supplied; another parameter variant does not implement a blocked feed.

## How to judge this lesson

Audit the next proposed retries for these lineages. Each should either be declined before replay spending or carry the comparison and a falsifiable expected difference. After an approved run, compare that prediction with the actual trade/input differences. An unexplained repeat of the old summary stops further retries pending diagnosis. Qualification gates remain unchanged.
