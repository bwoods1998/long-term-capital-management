# Bind replay passes to exact candidates

## What happened

- **hawkins-9:** one replay passed with 32 trades, +11.65% reported return, and deflated Sharpe **0.516974** against 17 trials. Other supplied records under that name failed with **zero trades**, then **one trade** and deflated Sharpe 0.041221. Its league record has **zero forward blocks**.
- **meriwether-28:** one replay passed with 40 trades, +11.35835% reported return, and deflated Sharpe **0.578707** against 15 trials. Other records failed with 16 trades and −9.52695%, and with two trades and +0.7928%. It also has **zero forward blocks**.
- **meriwether-12 and meriwether-16:** displaced after 11 and six agent-local replay trials, spending $1.00 and $1.05 respectively, with **zero forward blocks**. Their final replays failed the thresholds recorded at the time.

These deaths were displacement, not demonstrated forward trading losses. The records do not identify the code, parameters, tape, or evaluation window responsible for each result. They therefore do **not** establish that a passing implementation deteriorated, or that subsequent research caused inactivity. They establish that an agent name alone cannot identify qualified evidence.

## Before spending another research credit

1. **Locate the passing artifact.** Record its code/version, parameters, complete NEEDS, replay interval and coverage, execution assumptions, trial count, and policy threshold. Use existing archived inputs/results; mark unavailable fields unknown rather than inventing them.
2. **Keep evaluation contexts separate.** Determine whether the failing record used another candidate, another window, or the same setup. A development pass and an adverse independent test must both remain visible. Do not discard the adverse test simply because a pass exists.
3. **Check the adopted candidate.** Confirm what the House actually adopted and whether it matches the identified artifact. A retained proposal, agent name, or rung is not that confirmation. If provenance is unresolved, make artifact reconciliation—not another parameter sweep—the next task.
4. **Make the next observation attributable.** Where existing gates permit, hold the identified candidate fixed for forward observation. Record eligible opportunities, submissions, fills, completed exposures, and after-fee results. If nothing completes, identify the opportunity, settlement, data, or execution blocker before changing the signal. Do not force trades to manufacture activity.

## How to judge the change

The next research decision must name an exact candidate and explain the conflicting records. Success is attributable new forward evidence, or a specific documented blocker—not another historical pass. No new capital entitlement follows from this lesson; current replay, paper, risk, and live-capital gates remain in force.
