# What a larger research task exposed

September 13, 2026. The twelve-stage [financial dossier](RESEARCH-DOSSIER.md)
completed every provider request for an estimated **$0.661225284**. Its final
revision failed the report contract and remains unpublished. A separate two-call
follow-up cost **$0.167636960**. Original attempts, grades, and spending remain intact.

The measured export (`experiments/public/research-dossier.json`) contains the original stage results;
the follow-up export (`experiments/public/dossier-followup.json`) preserves its separate outcome.
The independent review (`experiments/data/experiments/dossier-review-2026-09-13.json`) separates
format, numerical, source-coverage, and substantive failures. These are authored
development tasks, not a held-out benchmark or investment-performance measurement.

The nine graded reports contain 299 repeated metric checks, not 299 independent
facts. Their stored grades give 297 numerical passes, 216 source-coverage passes,
and 215 passes on all required dimensions. The three invalid reports remain
outside those denominators, while their costs remain included.

## A concrete evidence-handoff failure

The final revision lost the passage reporting Azure growth. It explicitly described
zero as a schema placeholder for unavailable evidence, then returned an empty
citation. Zero was neither the disclosed value nor a valid representation of
unknown. The source existed in the original captured corpus; later stages had
received only passages cited by their parents.

The follow-up froze a different evidence policy **before** either new request:
both the revised report and its critic received all eleven verified review
passages plus parent-cited passages. The instruction also distinguished missing
evidence from zero and required unexplained reconciliation gaps to remain open.
No reference numerical answers were sent. This changed both context and
instructions; one follow-up cannot isolate their individual effects.

The revised report passed 62/64 strict checks, with all numerical values, units,
and periods correct. Its remaining two failures were RPO source-coverage
omissions. The critic passed 64/64. That is a useful recovery observation, but
independent review still found unsupported prose—including the critic's suggestion
that management used a different quarterly free-cash-flow definition. The supplied
quarterly figures reconcile exactly under operating cash flow less cash PP&E.
The automated score does not approve either report for publication.

## Useful financial findings

Operating cash flow rose by $46,773 million, while cash PP&E spending rose by
$51,397 million. The defined company-wide cash remainder therefore fell by
$4,624 million to $66,987 million. The complete nine-row operating asset/liability
group improved by only $455 million; selected positive contributions do not explain
most of the cash-flow increase. The bridge reconciles reported amounts without
establishing which benefits will recur or what AI-only returns were.
[Microsoft's annual cash-flow statement](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2026-q4/press-release-webcast)

Management's quarterly rounded cash PP&E and finance-lease figures sum to
$41.4 billion versus reported capex of $41 billion. Preserve that discrepancy
without assigning an unsupported cause. Demand growth and contracted backlog
also do not supply the incremental AI cash flows and invested capital needed to
calculate AI-specific returns.

The public [cash-flow explorer](https://blakewoods.us/portfolio/#cashflow) uses
independently checked issuer figures. It is separate from the unapproved model
drafts. [Lesson 7](../lessons/07-financial-dossier.md) lets you reproduce the bridge
without buying another request.

## The next useful improvement

Preserve the required evidence through every stage and give unavailable values an
explicit typed representation. Separate source extraction, arithmetic, and prose
review so a correct number cannot license an unsupported explanation. Then test
that contract on a newly curated disclosure and across real publication dates.
Repeated analysis of the same two sources does not establish long-horizon investing
ability, even when the workflow is durable and inexpensive.
