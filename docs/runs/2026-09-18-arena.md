The owner asked whether the approach had bugs. It had four that cost money and several that
cost evidence:

   cap and the entries had spent the $6,000 day, so 80 exit orders were rejected and two shorts

   `X-LTCM-Purpose: exit` skips the dollar caps (never the order count); caps $2,000 an order,
   $60,000 a day, 6,000 orders. Both shorts covered within a minute of the deploy (-$6 each).
2. **Explorer churn.** 46 explorers were installed and replaced in seven hours, ten minutes old

   under `protect_hours` (6) never makes room, and when the live book is full the candidate goes

   short ever produced a `desk.outcome`: the perp strategies had no record to size on or be
