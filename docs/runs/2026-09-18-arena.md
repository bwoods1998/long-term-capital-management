## The throttle (18:00-18:20 UTC)

The owner: "feels like it's getting worse... we need to do more and move faster." The honest
answer was to find why nothing had ever qualified in 270 Foundry cycles. The research cache
had it: a ten-day favorites replay sees 8,000 of 736,000 settled markets, a seeded draw spread
evenly over events "never by volume" (a rule written on Sept 16 against a real bias: ranked by
lifetime volume the winning bucket of an event is the one that traded most). One market in
ninety, and the favorites' universe is the liquid one percent, so a replay filled 8 to 17 times
in ten days against the live books' dozens a day; the gate asks for 60 trades and 25 out of
sample. The replay-to-real-money path was dead by construction.

The fix keeps the bias out and lets the liquid board in: the draw takes whole events from the
series that traded most over the window (a series' volume is summed over every bucket of every
event, so no bucket is preferred over its siblings), events within a series in a seeded order.
`family_board_draw: {"kalshi": "series_volume"}`. The first cycles under it tell whether the
favorites candidates now reach the gate.
