# The faster, economically accountable arena

Implemented September 17, 2026, during a one-hour build starting 03:56 UTC.

The objective is **positive real trading P&L after fees, slippage and Sail costs**, not more
orders, longer reasoning transcripts, attractive simulated returns or greater compute usage.
This release improves the mechanism for searching for that result. It does not establish alpha
or promise compounding returns. The floor was losing money before and after this release.

## Architecture now running

```text
Kalshi / Coinbase feeds + reconciled fills
                    |
        append-only, hash-chained event journal
           /                |                 \
 live/shadow books     settled evidence     public event pump
 + parallel quotes    grouped by event      independent of tick
           |                |                 |
 deterministic exits   Mind + Foundry       live website
 + risk + gateway      bounded wakeups      research/test/learn
           |                |
        live venue     parallel generation
                            |
                    first-ready candidate tests
                    in isolated Sail sandboxes
                            |
                    fixed validation + holdout
                            |
                    shadow forward evidence
                            |
                    gated live promotion
                            |
                      fills / settlements
```

The order/risk path remains serialized where ownership matters. Quote reads and independent
research work are parallel. The existing eight Foundry sandboxes and twelve strategy workers
remain bounded; raising expenditure alone is not a throughput strategy.

### Faster useful feedback

- Indexed desk-specific ledger replay avoids replaying every thought and unrelated fill.
- Whole-floor marks fetch one quote per instrument, with six concurrent readers. Exit quote
  reads also use six workers; exposure-changing exit submissions remain sequential.
- Completed strategy generations start testing immediately instead of waiting for the slowest
  model. Invalid generated code gets one bounded repair attempt, then the same frozen validator.
- A crypto family whose live strategies are all paused remains eligible for isolated research.
  Pausing a losing live strategy no longer removes its family from the research rotation. This
  does not reenable the live parent: qualified shadow successors still face existing promotion
  rules, including the rule against fast-tracking a mutation over an already paused parent.
- Four new independent outcome clusters can wake Foundry after ten minutes and Mind after five
  minutes. Normal periodic cycles remain as a fallback. Existing spending limits still apply.
- A separate two-second event pump sends public events without waiting for a slow floor tick or
  checkpoint. `lab.progress` exposes safe research, testing, candidate and learning milestones.
  Private model output, credentials and unreleased order intent remain private.

### Correct rewards and lifecycle

- Live and shadow allocations, fills and marks are folded separately. Promotion cannot import
  simulated profits. Demotion cannot erase historical real losses.
- The main P&L uses all real-money books, including demoted desks. Negative desk equity/cash is
  displayed honestly rather than clipped to zero. Venue balances remain separately identified.
- Live position entry/fee reconstruction filters out shadow history as well.
- Capital admission requires independent settled evidence. Related strikes and partial exits
  are aggregated, including their losses and fees, instead of counted as independent wins.
  Whole-desk admission uses the configured stronger confidence threshold; this is not a claim
  that all strategy-selection tests are statistically independent or free of selection bias.
- Retired ranges/weather families cannot silently restart through bootstrap, sessions, strategy
  installation or evolution. Explicit shadow controls remain available. Existing positions and
  risk-checked exits are preserved; the release does not force liquidation.
- Reconciliation runs immediately when no successful persisted result exists, and its successful
  timestamp survives a restart. Failed calls do not suppress retries for another hour.
- Sail infrastructure cost comes from cumulative project billing, cached five minutes, instead
  of summing overlapping rolling 24-hour windows. Last-known billing survives provider errors.
  Model costs remain separately recorded. The site shows trading P&L minus total Sail cost.

### Independent execution boundary

The external gateway still enforces its existing per-order and daily notional limits. A runtime
credential can engage the kill switch but cannot clear it. Clearing requires the owner-only
credential provisioned by `scripts/gateway_admin.py`, stored locally under `.data/ltcm/keys/`
with mode 0600 and in the gateway secret store, never on the trading VM.

Coinbase limit orders cannot understate notional with a cheaper reference header. Market orders
specified in base units get an independent public venue price plus buffer. An ambiguous network
failure after dispatch retains its cap reservation rather than pretending no order could exist.

## Observations, not forecasts

- Read-only production replay of eight live books over 53,873 events: 6.3124 seconds through the
  generic tape reader versus 0.1579 seconds through the indexed reader, with identical cash,
  equity, fees, deposits and positions. Approximately **40x faster for this replay workload**.
- A browser-equivalent WebSocket sample received research milestones 0.386–1.849 seconds after
  their event timestamps. This is a sample, not an end-to-end latency SLA for orders or all events.
- Foundry cycle 10 completed 14 backtests, with zero engine errors, in 352.6 wall-clock seconds;
  aggregate sandbox execution was 1,853.5 seconds. Model cost was $0.26575. **Zero candidates
  qualified**, so none was deployed. That is a valid research outcome, not a profitable result.
- Venue reconciliation at 04:20 UTC matched 36 Kalshi and two Coinbase positions with no
  mismatches. Journal integrity was rechecked after deployment, with over 53,000 events intact.
- At approximately 04:41 UTC the public tape showed trading P&L of -$101.92, model spend $46.89,
  infrastructure spend $1.38, and total Sail spend $48.28 (independent display rounding).
  Accounting corrections explain part of the headline change; it is not all newly incurred loss.

## Remaining constraints

- The floor is not profitable yet. Forward outcomes and settled cash, not speed benchmarks, must
  determine whether future strategies deserve more capital or compute.
- Thin Kalshi shard cash was producing warnings. Existing automatic collateral policy remains;
  the build does not move fresh owner funds into the system.
- A finalized-market sweep logged an `EventConflict`, and an event-index sweep logged a data
  error. Historical journal rows were not rewritten. These require continued reconciliation and
  targeted investigation; a clean position reconciliation does not prove every outcome is scored.
- Public checkpoints can trim shadow positions to meet the site's payload limit. Event streaming
  continues independently; the checkpoint is not an exhaustive shadow-position export.
- The main loop still has polling and sequential administrative work. This is not an HFT engine.
  Model/network latency, market liquidity, settlement delays and limited independent evidence
  remain real constraints. Graceful restarts can take minutes while in-flight work drains.
- The standalone historical research report still has legacy aggregate-book semantics; the live
  headline and runtime compute-allocation feedback use the segregated books. Do not treat a
  historical simulation/profile report as an audited statement of live earnings.
