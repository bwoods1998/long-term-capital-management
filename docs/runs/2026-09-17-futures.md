# Coinbase derivatives: the floor trades CDE perpetual futures

Built September 17, 2026, 22:45-23:30 UTC, after the owner's derivatives approval. Access was
confirmed through the gateway first: `cfm/balance_summary` showed $485.68 of futures buying
power, no positions, and the venue listed 100 CDE contracts (nano BTC perp 0.01 BTC, nano ETH
perp 0.1 ETH, SOL 5, XRP 500, AVAX 10, and dated contracts).

## Why futures, and not more spot

At the account's tier spot costs 0.5% maker / 0.9% taker. A live measurement at 22:40 UTC put
exactly one of the 80 most traded USD products at a spread wider than the fee-adjusted round
trip (1.21%), so no spot quoting strategy can earn here. CDE futures charge per contract (about
twenty cents, UNVERIFIED until the first fill), a few hundredths of a percent of a $75-800
contract, and a short is as easy as a long. The reversion signals that lost after spot fees are
worth testing again on the contracts.

## What was built

- **Instrument model.** A future is `Instrument("future", "ETP-20DEC30-CDE", "coinbase",
  multiplier=contract_size, expiry=...)`, quantity in whole contracts. The runtime's risk engine,
  ledger and shadow book already priced everything through the multiplier, so notional, P&L,
  shorts and flips work without accounting changes. `tools.instrument_from` takes the multiplier
  from the venue's listing (`contract_size_resolver`), never from the caller.
- **Adapter.** `positions()` adds `cfm/positions` (contracts signed by side, venue entry as
  cost); `balance()` adds the CFM wallet and its unrealized P&L and never a contract's notional;
  `submit` accepts a CDE product in whole contracts; `fee_rates` carries `future_contract`.
- **Risk.** `rule_short` allows a short only on a future: spot and event contracts are sold from
  a holding or not at all, whatever the venue lists. Coinbase's capabilities now include `short`.
- **Gateway.** A CDE order's notional is contracts x contract size x price, with the size read
  from the venue's product listing; an order whose size the venue does not list is refused.
- **Data.** Coinbase quotes and candles for CDE products; the composite routes a Coinbase future
  to Coinbase, not the equity source; the market socket skips CDE ids (support unverified) and
  their quotes come by REST.
- **Strategy.** `perp_reversion`: the perps a desk can hold one contract of (by notional and the
  desk's 60% position cap), z-score of the last five-minute close against 36 bars, enter one
  contract against a two-sigma move at the touch, long or short, target the mean, stop 1.2%
  beyond, four-hour time stop, all kept by the floor's exit engine; entry only when the move
  clears two contract fees, the spread and a 0.2% margin. Third house starter of the crypto
  family; six dealt variants on the shadow desks (`EXTRA_VARIANTS`).
- **Manifests.** Every crypto desk: asset classes crypto + future, `allow_short`, 60% position
  and order caps (Hilibrand in the repo; the children on the box).

## Not done

- Replays (23:15 UTC): the backtest simulator now trades CDE contracts from a table of the
  perps and their sizes (`backtest.PERPS`): whole contracts, a multiplier, shorts that open
  without a holding and never flip, per-contract fees, a short's stop above and target below,
  covered at the ask. The Foundry can replay `perp_reversion` over the crypto family's 21-day
  window. A contract not in the table cannot be simulated.
- Throughput (23:20 UTC): strategy dispatch left the tick. `Strategies.start_dispatcher` runs
  the due strategies every `dispatch_seconds` (15) on its own thread with 48 workers; the tick
  collects and promotes. Before this, 24 desks a tick with a four-minute tick meant a five-minute
  strategy ran every 25 minutes or more, and the perps starter had not run once in ten minutes.
  The tick's print routing (two minutes at 70 shadow books) now reads each book's quotes once.
- Funding payments on the perps are not folded into the ledger; they show up in the venue's
  balance and in reconciliation, not in a position's P&L.
- Attached brackets on a futures order are unverified; the floor's own exit engine keeps the
  stop, the target and the time stop.

## Live verification

- 23:20 UTC: the first perps intents (BCP short on Hilibrand VI, +2.33 sigma) were refused and
  nothing said why: `propose_order` defaulted a future to a `day` order. Fixed (Coinbase orders
  default to GTC) and the run now records refusal reasons on its row and its `desk.code_run`.
- 23:31 UTC: the first futures orders went through the whole path on two shadow desks
  (Hilibrand VI and IX): intent, risk approval, shadow fill. The live desk (Hilibrand III, $263,
  four contracts in its universe) waits for a two-sigma move it can hold.
- Tick after the dispatcher and the drain fix: about 90 seconds (feeds phase gone from the
  timing), strategies phase under a second, the dispatcher running every 15 s.
- 23:58 UTC: Hilibrand III's own model read its strategy report, judged `perp_reversion`
  off-mandate (the mandate named BTC and ETH spot) and undeployed it; bootstrap runs once per
  process, so the row was gone until the next restart. Every crypto desk's mandate now names
  the CDE perps and the house perps strategy (box manifests and the repo founder).
- 00:15 UTC: the first shadow perps round trip closed: Hilibrand XIII sold BCP at 235.35 and
  covered at 233.72 on the target, through the floor's own exit engine.
- 00:35 UTC: live desks run the perps at a 1.5-sigma trigger, two entries a run, contracts to
  $300 at a 100% position cap (`EXTRA_LIVE_PARAMS`); a house row stamped by a code refresh
  follows the house params again (its params had been frozen after the first refresh).
- 01:03 UTC: the first live perps intent was refused as fractional: the learning-size cap had
  turned one contract into $25 worth. Futures now cap in whole contracts with a floor of one.
- 01:51-02:01 UTC: three live intents reached the gateway and were refused with "Cannot
  independently price this order": the Worker's bare fetch of the venue's product listing
  failed. The fetch now names itself and its failures name the venue status.
- **02:06:54 UTC: the first real Coinbase futures trade.** Hilibrand III sold one
  POP-20DEC30-CDE (100 DOT) at 1.112, commission $0.2312 (the estimate was $0.20). A BCP order
  in the same run was refused for "Too many decimals in order price": perps price on
  `price_increment` (BCP 0.1), which the kit now reports.
- 02:08 UTC: the fill was keyed as spot with a multiplier of 1, so the ledger booked a hundredth
  of the position under a key the exit engine could not find, and the account read a $68 loss
  because the spot wallet's available USD had dropped by the venue's $65 margin hold. Fixed
  (`CoinbaseBroker.instrument_for`; equity from the venue's `total_usd_balance`), the tape
  repaired with two corrective fills (`repair-cde-key-1-undo/redo`), and at 02:20 UTC the
  ledger showed the short at 100 DOT (−$0.20 unrealized), the account $913.22, the exit plan
  armed (stop 1.12, target 1.09, time stop 06:06 UTC).
- Throughput in the same hour: with 122k events and 586 exit plans the tick reached 12 minutes
  and the site froze for an hour. A `(stream, kind, seq)` index, a memo for held positions'
  entries, a fold-read cache invalidated by appends, one TLS context per process and no quotes
  for time-stop-only plans brought the tick to about eight seconds. Restarts no longer sleep
  the sandboxes first.

## The rest of the night (03:00-04:00 UTC)

Fixes found by watching the loop after the futures went live, each deployed with a green suite:

- The Foundry's research sandboxes were leased for 25 minutes after every restart; the leases
  are released at start (`release_leases_on_start`), and the loop exits at once on a restart
  (the interpreter had been joining the Foundry's backtest threads for minutes).
- Its model stage had no deadline (`model_wait_seconds`, 600); a lost call had held a cycle.
- Replays list the ten liquid perps over seven days (21 days of five-minute candles for 22
  contracts timed out with no trades), fill on the touch (`fill_model`), and skip spot quoting
  (`excluded_strategies`), whose replay makes no trade at any spread at this fee tier.
- The venue balance read gets twelve seconds (four calls now); the event index sweep and the
  provider's stored errors carry the venue's message, which showed the recurring
  `provider_http_400` to be "idempotency_key already exists for a different request": a
  resumed turn's body differs from the key's first use. Such a call is resent once under a key
  that names its body.
- The public balance chart: a later mark may void earlier marks with a reason; seven marks
  from before the audited opening, from reads that missed held USD, and from the margin hold
  were voided, and the chart starts at the audited opening (976.11).
- The gateway's product lookup for futures orders is signed and cached a minute after the
  venue rate-limited the bare fetch and refused a live order.
