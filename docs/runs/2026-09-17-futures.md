# Coinbase derivatives: the floor trades CDE perpetual futures

Built September 17, 2026, 22:45-23:30 UTC, after the owner's derivatives approval. Access was
confirmed through the gateway first: `cfm/balance_summary` showed $485.68 of futures buying
power, no positions, and the venue listed 100 CDE contracts (nano BTC perp 0.01 BTC, nano ETH
perp 0.1 ETH, SOL 5, XRP 500, AVAX 10, and dated contracts).

## Why futures, and not more spot

At the account's tier spot costs 0.5% maker / 0.9% taker. A live measurement at 22:40 UTC put
exactly one of the 80 most traded USD products at a spread wider than the fee-adjusted round
trip (1.21%), so no spot quoting strategy can earn here. CDE futures charge per contract (about
twenty cents, UNVERIFIED until the first fill), a few hundredths of a percent of a $75-800
contract, and a short is as easy as a long. The reversion signals that lost after spot fees are
worth testing again on the contracts.

## What was built

- **Instrument model.** A future is `Instrument("future", "ETP-20DEC30-CDE", "coinbase",
  multiplier=contract_size, expiry=...)`, quantity in whole contracts. The runtime's risk engine,
  ledger and shadow book already priced everything through the multiplier, so notional, P&L,
  shorts and flips work without accounting changes. `tools.instrument_from` takes the multiplier
  from the venue's listing (`contract_size_resolver`), never from the caller.
- **Adapter.** `positions()` adds `cfm/positions` (contracts signed by side, venue entry as
  cost); `balance()` adds the CFM wallet and its unrealized P&L and never a contract's notional;
  `submit` accepts a CDE product in whole contracts; `fee_rates` carries `future_contract`.
- **Risk.** `rule_short` allows a short only on a future: spot and event contracts are sold from
  a holding or not at all, whatever the venue lists. Coinbase's capabilities now include `short`.
- **Gateway.** A CDE order's notional is contracts x contract size x price, with the size read
  from the venue's product listing; an order whose size the venue does not list is refused.
- **Data.** Coinbase quotes and candles for CDE products; the composite routes a Coinbase future
  to Coinbase, not the equity source; the market socket skips CDE ids (support unverified) and
  their quotes come by REST.
- **Strategy.** `perp_reversion`: the perps a desk can hold one contract of (by notional and the
  desk's 60% position cap), z-score of the last five-minute close against 36 bars, enter one
  contract against a two-sigma move at the touch, long or short, target the mean, stop 1.2%
  beyond, four-hour time stop, all kept by the floor's exit engine; entry only when the move
  clears two contract fees, the spread and a 0.2% margin. Third house starter of the crypto
  family; six dealt variants on the shadow desks (`EXTRA_VARIANTS`).
- **Manifests.** Every crypto desk: asset classes crypto + future, `allow_short`, 60% position
  and order caps (Hilibrand in the repo; the children on the box).

## Not done

- Replays (23:15 UTC): the backtest simulator now trades CDE contracts from a table of the
  perps and their sizes (`backtest.PERPS`): whole contracts, a multiplier, shorts that open
  without a holding and never flip, per-contract fees, a short's stop above and target below,
  covered at the ask. The Foundry can replay `perp_reversion` over the crypto family's 21-day
  window. A contract not in the table cannot be simulated.
- Throughput (23:20 UTC): strategy dispatch left the tick. `Strategies.start_dispatcher` runs
  the due strategies every `dispatch_seconds` (15) on its own thread with 48 workers; the tick
  collects and promotes. Before this, 24 desks a tick with a four-minute tick meant a five-minute
  strategy ran every 25 minutes or more, and the perps starter had not run once in ten minutes.
  The tick's print routing (two minutes at 70 shadow books) now reads each book's quotes once.
- Funding payments on the perps are not folded into the ledger; they show up in the venue's
  balance and in reconciliation, not in a position's P&L.
- Attached brackets on a futures order are unverified; the floor's own exit engine keeps the
  stop, the target and the time stop.
