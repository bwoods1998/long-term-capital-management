PREREG-EVL-7, PART L: TWO PRE-REGISTERED ONLINE LEARNERS (draft text to freeze; lens "learner", synthetic calibration only)

Designed blind: from the frozen PREREG-EVL-1..-5, the EVL-6 draft and its judge's synthetic search, the code of
rel/1019e..g, the outcome-free counts in speed/SPEED-FACTS.md and EXECUTION-FACTS.md, the literature, and the synthetic
Monte Carlo evl7/learner/calib_learner.py. No price, return, look, band, P&L or bar of any event or program was read.

L0. WHAT A LEARNER IS. A seatable program whose rule is a FIXED ALGORITHM: at each decision it chooses at most K = 8
events from a fixed candidate pool by a model fit at that decision only on outcomes SETTLED AND STORED BEFORE it
(outcomes.status = 'settled' and outcomes.settled_at < tau). Its features, algorithm, hyperparameters, warm-up, pool,
universe, ranking and constants are fixed here, before any of its events exists; none is chosen per session, per result
or per program. Using settled outcomes at run time is the forward test itself: each pick is made before its own outcome
exists. A learner's RECORD counts only events knowable after REGISTERED_AT_7 (it registers after REGISTERED_AT_6, A91's
method); its TRAINING rows may be older (every settled pool outcome stored before the decision).

L1. THE PROGRAMS (version 1 each, seatable at PREREG-EVL-3's learning band from birth):
  EVL-LRN-O1: entry OPEN of D, exit CLOSE of D (hold 1); tau = D's open decision (09:15 ET); POOL, in this order:
    EVL-CTRL-NEWS-ALL-O1, EVL-CTRL-8K-ALL-O1, EVL-CTRL-ERN-ALL-O1, EVL-INS-BUY-O1 (all version 1).
  EVL-LRN-ON: entry CLOSE of D, exit OPEN of D+1 (hold 1); tau = D's close decision (close - 14 min); POOL, in order:
    EVL-CTRL-NEWS-DAY-ON, EVL-CTRL-NEWSPM-ON, EVL-CTRL-8K-ON, EVL-ERN-REACT-ON, EVL-ERN-DROP-ON, EVL-CTRL-ERN-UNDER-ON
    (all version 1; a pool program not yet live contributes nothing).
  Free records (derived controls, no intents and no settlement of their own): EVL-LRN-O1-POOL, EVL-LRN-ON-POOL = every
  candidate of each decision of their learner (L2 (i)-(ii), before its one-position skip and hurdle) with the pool
  outcome of its (D, issuer) (L4).

L2. CANDIDATES at tau. For each pool program p, in pool order: the events p's own rule takes for entry session D from
rows STORED before tau with verdicts ANSWERED before tau and the LIVE rule's universe rows only (PREREG-EVL-1 section
3's real-money reading, as league/events/book.py `picks(lane, p, D, decided_at=tau)` makes it, p's own one-position rule
included; a verdict not answered before tau reads as no score). One CANDIDATE per issuer: its POOL FAMILIES are the pool
programs selecting it; its PRIMARY EVENT is the event of the first of them in pool order (the learner's intent's event).
Kept only when (i) TRADABLE at decision (1.25 x prev_close <= 10% of the equity at the previous close), (ii) for picks,
its primary event is knowable after REGISTERED_AT_7, and (iii) for picks, the learner holds no intent of the issuer
entered before D that exits within the last 5 sessions or later (PREREG-EVL-1 section 5 as A72 reads it).

L3. FEATURES x (each read as stored before tau; a missing input is 0 with its own indicator; definitions, order and
scaling never change for a version). COMMON: dummies of the primary pool program (the first is the baseline); multi (>= 2
pool programs select the issuer); from the best `ok` verdict answered before tau among its pool events (PROMPT-NEWS for
news, PROMPT-DOC for 8-K): s_num = s/3, s>=2, s=3, s<=-1, no-score, and s_num x each primary dummy; PROMPT-DOC guidance
raised, lowered; 8-K items 1.01, 2.01, 7.01, 8.01; src_benzinga (the primary article's source contains "benzinga");
n_art = ln(1 + single-symbol articles of the ticker knowable in the primary family's window); n_att20 = ln(1 + such
articles in the 20 sessions before that window); first20 (in none of this learner's candidate sets of the previous 20
sessions); mon, fri (first / last session of the calendar week); issuer (D's live universe row, cik_info): ln(adv20/1e7),
ln(prev_close/20), ln(sig20/0.03) with sig20 the sd of the 19 daily log changes of the 20 stored raw SIP closes before D
(floor 0.005, cap 0.25), bench_iwm, SIC bio (2833-2836, 8731), tech (3570-3579, 3600-3699, 7370-7379), fin (6000-6799),
energy (1000-1499, 2900-2999); r1 = clip(ln(c-1/c-2), 0.5), r5 = clip(ln(c-1/c-6), 1), r20 = clip(ln(c-1/c-20), 1.5)
from the same closes (c-1 = prev_close). EVL-LRN-O1 ADDS: the primary event's knowable hour (ET) overnight [20:00, 04:00),
pre [04:00, 07:00), late [07:00, 09:00); ln(1 + purchase value / $50,000) of an EVL-INS-BUY-O1 event (D = 38).
EVL-LRN-ON ADDS: morning (knowable 09:00-12:00 ET of D), before (knowable before 09:00 ET of D); dmove = clip(ln(m1530 /
prev_close), 0.5) with m1530 the ticker's stored '1530' mark for D fetched before tau (the close pre-pass reads its
candidates' marks in MARK_BATCH requests), dmove missing, |dmove|, s_num x dmove (D = 43).

L4. TRAINING ROWS at tau: every candidate (s, i) of every earlier entry session s of the learner's kind, at most 250
sessions back (the candidate sets stored at its own decisions and, for sessions before REGISTERED_AT_7, the sets its
first run RECONSTRUCTS once as of each session's own tau_s by L2-L3), whose (s, issuer) has a POOL OUTCOME: an outcomes
row of a pool program for that issuer entering at s with the learner's auctions (the first in pool order), status
'settled', tradable 1, settled_at < tau. Label y = clip(a / sig20, -5, 5), a that row's after-cost abnormal return
(PREREG-EVL-1 section 5). A training row's features are the ones stored for it as of tau_s, never recomputed.

L5. THE FIT (session-demeaned weighted ridge), at each decision: w_s = 2^(-(n(D) - n(s)) / 40) (H = 40 sessions, n the
lane's session index); within each session s, x~ = x - mean_s x and y~ = y - mean_s y over its training rows (a session
with one row adds nothing: the day shock, the pool's drift and the benchmark cancel inside a session); A = sum_s w_s sum_i
x~ x~', b = sum_s w_s sum_i x~ y~, N_w = sum_s w_s n_s; d_j = sqrt(A_jj / N_w) (a column with d_j < 1e-9 gets 0);
M = diag(d)^-1 A diag(d)^-1 + 8000 I; beta = diag(d)^-1 M^-1 diag(d)^-1 b. Sums by math.fsum in (entry day, cik) order;
M solved by Cholesky in float64.
PREDICTION for candidate i of D: e_i = (x_i - xbar_D) . beta (xbar_D the mean over D's candidates kept by L2 (i));
c_i = the replay's round trip for i (PREREG-EVL-1 section 5: 2 x (0.5 + 5 or 10 bp by ADV) + $0.03 over 5% of the
previous close's equity); z_i = e_i - c_i / sig20_i (the LEVEL is the fixed prior that the pool's gross abnormal mean is
zero: never learned, so no pick can time a drift common to the pool); ahat_i = sig20_i x z_i, the predicted after-cost
abnormal return.
PICK: the candidates with z_i > 0, ranked by ahat_i (higher first), then adv20, then event id; the first 8 are the
learner's intents of D (rank_key = ahat_i). No other pick exists.

L6. WARM-UP AND FAILURE. No pick until the training set holds >= 2,000 rows from >= 3 sessions. A decision that cannot
read its pool, outcomes or features, or whose fit is not finite, picks nothing (fail closed) and says why. The learner's
part runs after every earlier row is decided, under its own bound (90 s at the open, 120 s at the close); its failure
never cuts an earlier row; that auction is then decided for the learner by itself, later, exactly as of tau (every cut-
off read from stored times: received_at, documents.stored_at, answered_at, marks.fetched_at, settled_at), shadow only,
never traded. A decision written is never decided again.

L7. WHAT IS WRITTEN (additive tables; SCHEMA_VERSION stays 1): learner_fits (program, version, day, tau, n_rows,
n_sessions, beta, d, xbar, the sha256 of the ordered training keys); learner_cands (program, version, day, cik, event,
families, features, e, z, ahat, picked, rank). The written picks are the record; a nightly recompute alarms on a mismatch
and never rewrites.

L8. REAL MONEY: PREREG-EVL-3 section 2's bands, the walls, the open-risk weight and the loss budget, unchanged. The book's
picks of a learner are its intents of that decision in rank order, less the book's own skips (a name given to an earlier
program at the auction, the sale gate, no whole share, cash, A85's reservation, the walls, the day's order line). ROOM:
after every PREREG-EVL-1..-6 row and before R3's births, EVL-LRN-O1 then EVL-LRN-ON. In real money only, EVL-LRN-ON does
not buy an issuer with an 8-K knowable at or after 09:00 ET of D whose PROMPT-DOC verdict is ok with score >= 2, or ok
with score >= 1 and guidance "raised", or not answered at tau (PREREG-EVL-4 and -5 section 1.3). The shadow keeps
every pick.

L9. THE JUDGE: PREREG-EVL-6 sections 1-3 (the seating screen of every version registered after REGISTERED_AT_6). A
learner is a READ program wherever a rule names one; its control is its derived POOL record, over its own block sessions.
Its record is its forward picks only. If PREREG-EVL-6 is not frozen, PREREG-EVL-3 sections 2-3 as PREREG-EVL-4 section 2
applies them, and each learner version is charged 0.16 (L11). The code never edits the screen that judges it.

L10. RE-ENTRY (pre-registered): when a learner version is RETIRED by its judge (lower-tail retirement, or EXHAUSTION at
its last look without a pass), version n + 1 of the same id and this rule unchanged is registered the first night
after through R3's registration route (registration time the later of GitHub's commit time and the House's receipt),
at most versions 1-3 per id, each counting only events knowable after its own registration and training on everything
settled before each decision. Each draws 0.10 at registration and is refused when the lane's budget cannot hold it.

L11. FALSE PASSES: 0.10 a learner version under PREREG-EVL-6 (0.20 for the two version 1 rows: 1.5 + 0.2 = 1.7 of the
stated 3.0; at most 0.60 with every re-entry); 0.16 a version under PREREG-EVL-3 (no re-entry there).
MEASURED (calib_learner.py, final design, per version, the EVL-6 judge's two leading 10%-cap candidates L12 / E840):
  zero-edge base (rho_day .05, t3): 3.00% / 3.15%; random picker of the same size 3.1% / 3.0% (first design).
  worst plausible (rho_day .15, crash days, left-skewed events, 20 bp AR .97 pool drift, day momentum .10), no issuer
  effect: 7.30% / 7.40% (pool 300), 7.70% / 6.90% (pool 800), 8.33% / 8.33% over 300 sessions; with a 1% persistent issuer
  effect 7.45% / 7.70% (that effect is learnable: picks +2.7 bp forward).
  knife edge (features rank the pool, the best 5% have mean exactly 0): 4.3-5.8% / 4.3-6.3%.
  three versions chained (L10): 8.75% / 8.70% base, 22.05% / 21.40% worst, 23.5% / 22.7% over 300 sessions (charged 0.30).
  under PREREG-EVL-3 (its skew check omitted, an upper bound): 5.85% base, 14.00% worst, 16.0% over 300 sessions.
  named stresses: rho_day .30 8.7% / 9.1%; 30 bp drift 7.8% / 8.1%; rho_day .30 with day momentum .40 16.3% / 17.1%
  (a day-shock property shared by every program); a learnable family drift 10.0% / 10.9% (picks +12 bp forward).

L12. REPORTED BESIDE (never a judge): per learner and decision, candidates, picks, warm-up state, training rows; after
settlement the Spearman correlation of z with a across the candidates (realised IC), the picks' mean a minus the pool's,
their running means (the learning curve). These and the fit tables are outcome-derived: R3's facts authorizer refuses
learner_fits and learner_cands.

L13. NOT DECIDED HERE: the code (league/events/learner.py; run.py's part; book.py's rows; screen.py's derived control and
registries; programs.py's rows and REGISTERED_AT_7; births.py's authorizer and re-entry line; the report). An ambiguity
takes the reading less likely to pass (screen) or that trades less (trading), fixed before the first settled event and
recorded as an amendment.
