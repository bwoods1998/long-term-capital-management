PREREG-EVL-9 CLAUSES FROM THE LEAKAGE AND SURVIVORSHIP RED TEAM (to be pasted as written; every figure is from
evl9/redteam/rt_warm.report.txt or rt_skew.out*.jsonl)

R1 TIMES AND COUNTING.
(a) REGISTERED_AT_9 (T_F) is fixed by A92's three-clock method; the tag prereg-evl-9 never moves; the code sha it names
    holds the fetcher, the historical builder, the label, the trainer, the gates and the House scorer.
(b) S_CUT is the last NYSE session (league.equity.store.nyse_sessions) whose close is before T_F. No input of the model
    names a session, filing date, acceptance time or news time after S_CUT; every market-data request's end is the
    earlier of (S_CUT + 1 day) 00:00 America/New_York and now - 16 minutes.
(c) MODEL_AT (T_M) of a version is GitHub's commit time of docs/prereg/evl-9/model-<id>-v<n>.json, its MANIFEST digest
    and its gate receipts; the file's sha256 is pinned in the captain's amendment WARM_MODEL_<id>_v<n> in the same hour.
(d) COUNT_FROM_9 of a version = max(T_F, T_M). The version counts only events knowable after COUNT_FROM_9 (PREREG-EVL-1
    section 3), and no decision of it exists before COUNT_FROM_9. "Judged only on events after its registration" is
    read as "after COUNT_FROM_9", which is never earlier.
(e) The decisions from COUNT_FROM_9 to the first decision of the House release carrying the model are decided by that
    release, once each, oldest first, exactly as of each decision time with A72's stored-before cut-offs, in shadow
    only, with receipt kinds decide-open-warm:<day> and decide-close-warm:<day>; none is ever traded and none is
    decided again.
(f) If T_M is not on GitHub within 96 hours of T_F the version is VOID (R8).

R2 THE VINTAGE.
(a) Inputs that carry no price (SEC submissions.zip, the SEC Insider Transactions Data Sets, the browse-edgar Atom
    acceptance times, Alpaca news metadata with include_content=false) may be fetched before T_F; inputs that carry a
    price (Alpaca 1Day bars raw and adjustment=all, 1Min bars, auctions) are fetched only after T_F. Every response is
    stored compressed under the vault (~/Work/.ltcm-main/evl9-vault/<T_F>/) with its request, fetch time and sha256 in
    MANIFEST.jsonl; the model is built from the vault only, never from a live re-fetch.
(b) ALLOWED fields: SEC filings by their own filingDate <= S_CUT, form exactly "8-K" or "4" (no amendments), the 8-K
    items field; the Atom <updated> acceptance time; Form 4 non-derivative transactions of original filings by their own
    FILING_DATE; ISSUERTRADINGSYMBOL as the symbol of its issuer as of that filing; raw daily bars for every level and
    adjustment=all only as a ratio of two days inside one window; asof only to fetch a point-in-time symbol; auction
    prints; news id, created_at, updated_at, source, headline, symbols; the NYSE calendar; the complete 10-K/10-Q dates.
(c) FORBIDDEN in the vault and the model (forward refits are R9's) as a feature, a filter, a label input, a weight or a
    reason a row exists: company_tickers.json and
    company_tickers_exchange.json; Alpaca asset status, tradable and exchange; the submissions files' current tickers,
    exchanges and former names; their acceptanceDateTime; any reader verdict or model call; restated XBRL comparatives (a fact is read only from the submission that
    first reports its period, at that submission's acceptance + 10 minutes); amended documents; any
    House outcome, mark or look of a session after S_CUT; any year-2020 session (league/xs/config.py RESERVE_YEARS).
    Current SIC is read only for PREREG-EVL-7 section 1.4 item 10's four groups and the 6221 exclusion.

R3 KNOWABLE TIMES OF HISTORY.
    8-K: the Atom <updated> + 10 minutes, else filingDate 17:30 ET + 10 minutes. Form 4: the issuer's owner=only Atom
    <updated> + 10 minutes, else FILING_DATE 22:00 ET + 10 minutes. News: created_at + 2 minutes, and an article whose
    updated_at is more than 10 minutes after created_at is excluded. A decision for session D reads daily bars of
    sessions before D only; the '1530' mark is league/events/run.py `_marks_1530`'s rule, knowable at 15:46 ET. Every
    feature row records inputs_at, the latest time of any input it read.

R4 THE LABEL.
    At the version's own entry and exit auctions, the listing exchange's auction print (v2/stocks/auctions) and the
    benchmark's (SPY when adv20 >= $50M, else IWM) at the same auctions; prices adjusted by the day's adjusted/raw close
    ratio as league/events/score.py `_ratio`; an entry print above 1.25 x the previous raw close is skipped (PREREG-EVL-2);
    TRADABLE at E_REF = the account equity at the close before T_F. y = clip(a_g / sigma20, -5, 5) with the GROSS
    abnormal return a_g = exit/entry - bench_exit/bench_entry. The cost is charged once, at the
    pick hurdle, on the judge's basis for that event. A row with no auction print at entry or no exit print within 5
    sessions is dropped and counted by reason. If v2/stocks/auctions is not admitted by T_F, a closing print is the raw
    daily bar close (label_source 'bar', its share reported); no version entering or exiting at an opening auction is
    registered until it is admitted.

R5 FEATURES.
(a) No CIK, ticker or name indicator; no encoding of past labels, returns or reactions by issuer or by any group of fewer
    than 200 issuers; a tree learner's every leaf holds rows of at least 200 distinct issuers (rt_warm W-TE-tau1: an
    issuer encoding passes 94.75%, and 89.75% at the worst corner, where the lane's null holds a 1% persistent issuer effect).
(b) Counts and lengths (articles, symbols, filings, items, headline characters) enter as within-session percentile ranks.
(c) Form 4's 10b5-1 flag is not read.
(d) ONE function, league/events/warm/features.py `features(event, view, tau)`, computes every feature, over the vault
    (VaultView) for history and over the House store (StoreView) for decisions; both views expose the same methods.
(e) The House writes each candidate's features at its decision into warm_cands (program, version, day, tau, cik, event,
    inputs_at, features, e, z, ahat, eligible, picked, rank), write-once; nothing recomputes a written row.

R6 TRAINING.
    Years 2016-2019 and 2021 to S_CUT; each calendar year's rows carry equal total weight; rows of a (year, ALIVE or not) cell, ALIVE = the issuer filed any
    form in the 365 days before S_CUT (from the submissions file, filtered to filing dates <= S_CUT), whose coverage (rows
    kept / events) is more than 10 points below the year's other cell are weighted by the ratio of the two coverages;
    the registered inner procedure (era folds 2016-17, 2018-19, 2021, 2022-23, 2024-S_CUT; a column whose fold
    sign differs from its full-window sign in two or more folds is zeroed) runs inside the sealed run; nothing else is
    tuned. No in-sample statistic of any kind is computed. A version whose forward refits forget does so with a half-life
    of at least 120 sessions (rt_warm SEL-SECDRIFT30: half-lives 20 and 40 pass 10.7% and 10.5% under a 30 bp sector drift
    against 6.5% at 500).

R7 THE SEALED RUN.
    scripts/warm_train.py runs on blake-desktop only from the commit tagged prereg-evl-9, after T_F (it reads the tag
    with `git ls-remote` and refuses otherwise), with Python's standard library, math.fsum in (session, CIK) order and a
    float64 Cholesky; it prints and writes counts, hashes and the model file only. A second run from the vault must
    agree to 1e-12 relative in every number. The model file holds the column scaling, the coefficients, the sufficient
    statistics forward refits need, the zeroed columns, the sha256 of the ordered row keys, the MANIFEST sha256 and the
    code sha.

R8 GATES AND VOID.
    Before T_M, in order: G1 coverage (rows per training year >= 2,000; point-in-time symbol coverage >= 70% of each year's
    events; label coverage >= 80%; no coverage cell below 50%); G2 parity over the sessions both hold (Oct 19 to S_CUT):
    candidate sets Jaccard >= 0.95 per family, features equal to 1e-9 on >= 99% of rows, knowable times within 1 second
    on >= 99%; G3 leakage lint (static: no forbidden source in the code paths; dynamic: no vault session after S_CUT, no
    row with inputs_at >= its tau, no label exit after S_CUT, no 2020 row); G4 determinism; G5 finite fit. A failure
    makes the version VOID: never seated, its charge not drawn, the gate, its counts and the reason written in the
    amendments. At most two VOIDs an id; a third attempt draws its charge whatever happens. After T_M nothing voids a
    version.

R9 THE HOUSE.
    The House decides nothing for a version at a time before its COUNT_FROM_9, reads only a model whose sha256 is the
    pinned one and whose MANIFEST S_CUT precedes its first counted session, and fails closed (no picks, a receipt
    saying why) otherwise. Forward refits read only pool outcomes settled before tau, of entry sessions after S_CUT,
    with features as written at their own decisions.

R10 WHAT NOBODY READS.
    The vault's price files, every model file and every refit state are outcome-derived: no designer, architect brief,
    birth prompt or R3 facts file reads or cites them (R3's facts authorizer refuses their paths), and no report prints
    an in-sample figure. A captain read is written in the amendments with its time.

R11 CHANGES.
    Any change to the code, the data spec, a constant or the model after T_F is a new version with its own T_F, S_CUT,
    T_M and COUNT_FROM_9 and its own charge. Re-entry is PREREG-EVL-7 section 1.10's mechanical rule only; a re-entered
    version is re-trained from a new vault with the same code and counts only after its own COUNT_FROM_9.

R12 THE SHAPE GUARD (this file's versions only).
    A candidate whose sigma20 (the standard deviation of its 19 raw daily log changes before D) is below 1.5% is not
    eligible to be picked; it stays in the pool, the fit and the control. (rt_skew.py: a pick set made only of low-
    volatility steady-gain rare-crash events, every event's mean exactly 0, passes PREREG-EVL-6's drafted screen 32.35%
    at sigma 1%, 22.6% at 1-1.5%, 13.53% at 1.5-2.5% with a 1% chance of -30% a session; 6.13% at 0.5%.) The report
    shows, per look, the record's share of events with a below -15% beside the pool's.

R13 HONESTY.
    The designers, the judge and the checks read no outcome, return, price, bar, look, band or P&L of any event, symbol
    or program; only counts, code, documentation and synthetic simulations (rt_warm.py, rt_skew.py and the other lenses'
    scripts, copied with their sha256 into docs/prereg/evl-9/ at the freeze). The registering captain session has read
    per-program shadow means of the existing programs for Oct 19-20 and the lane's aggregate real-money figures, and
    earlier lanes' agents read index-ETF, options and S&P 500 cross-sectional outcomes for 2016-2019 and 2021-2024; the
    captain chose none of these rules, and every session up to S_CUT enters the training by rule, not by choice. Nobody
    computes or reads an in-sample figure before or after T_M.
