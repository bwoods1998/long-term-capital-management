PREREG-EVL-9, PART L: TWO WARM-STARTED LEARNERS (EVL-WL-O1, EVL-WL-ON). Draft text to freeze (lens: learners and
protocol). Designed blind from the frozen PREREG-EVL-1..-5, the drafts of PREREG-EVL-6, -7 and -8, the code of
origin/rel/1019g, the scout's record counts (evl9/scout/), the outcome-free facts files, the literature, and synthetic
Monte Carlo only (evl9/learners/calib_evl9.py). No price, return, look, band, bar or P&L of any event, symbol or
program, historical or live, was read.

W0. WHAT A WARM LEARNER IS, AND THE ORDER OF THINGS.
A seatable program whose rule is ONE FIXED PROCEDURE with three stages, every part of which this file fixes before any
of its events exists: TRAIN (a deterministic trainer fits a prior on a history table that a deterministic builder makes
from the data spec W4), LEARN FORWARD (at each decision the House refits on that prior plus pool outcomes settled and
stored before the decision), PICK (W6). The order is fixed: (1) THIS TEXT, with every constant, is committed and
registered (REGISTERED_AT_9, A92's method: the latest of the commit time, GitHub's activity time for the branch push and
the pushing client's clock after the tag push); counting starts here; (2) CODE_PIN: the commit sha and sha256s of
scripts/evl9/build_table.py, scripts/evl9/train.py and league/events/warm.py (its `features`, `gbt_eval` and `decide`)
are recorded in the captain's amendment BEFORE the first training run; the implementers read no outcome, price or label
(counts-only logs, synthetic tests); (3) the builder builds and the trainer trains, deterministically, and the job
writes TABLE_SHA and MODEL_SHA into the training receipt; (4) the House verifies MODEL_SHA and decides, the decisions
since REGISTERED_AT_9 first (W7). A program of this file counts only events knowable after REGISTERED_AT_9 (and after
its own version's registration). NOBODY READS ANY FIT OF THE HISTORY, in-sample or out-of-sample, before or after
registration: the receipt carries counts and hashes only. Any change after the CODE_PIN (a feature, a constant, a
source, a window, a builder fix that changes any row) is a new registration of a new version that counts only events knowable after it; a re-run of the SAME code on the SAME inputs is not a change.

W1. THE PROGRAMS, POOLS AND CANDIDATES.
- EVL-WL-O1 version 1: entry OPEN of D, exit CLOSE of D (hold 1, block D); tau = D's open decision time (09:15 ET).
  POOL, in order (each version 1): EVL-CTRL-NEWS-ALL-O1, EVL-CTRL-8K-ALL-O1, EVL-CTRL-ERN-ALL-O1, EVL-INS-BUY-O1.
- EVL-WL-ON version 1: entry CLOSE of D, exit OPEN of D+1 (hold 1, block D); tau = D's close decision time (close - 14
  min). POOL, in order: EVL-CTRL-NEWS-DAY-ON, EVL-CTRL-NEWSPM-ON, EVL-CTRL-8K-ON, EVL-ERN-REACT-ON, EVL-ERN-DROP-ON,
  EVL-CTRL-ERN-UNDER-ON. A pool program not yet live contributes nothing.
- Every pool program is VERDICT-FREE (rule none), so each pool has an exact history twin made of structured records.
- CANDIDATES at tau, KEPT, ELIGIBLE TO BE PICKED, PRIMARY EVENT, POOL OUTCOME: PREREG-EVL-7 draft section 1.1 and 1.3
  word for word, stated here so this file stands alone: for each pool program p in order, the events p's own rule takes
  for D as real money reads them (book.picks(lane, p, D, decided_at=tau)); ONE CANDIDATE PER ISSUER, its primary event
  the event of the first pool program selecting it; KEPT when TRADABLE at decision (1.25 x prev_close <= 10% of E_prev);
  ELIGIBLE TO BE PICKED when also its primary event is knowable after REGISTERED_AT_9 (and the version's own
  registration) and the learner holds no intent of the issuer entered before D that exits within the last 5 sessions or
  later, nor a written non-skipped intent of it entering within the 5 sessions after D. PREREG-EVL-2's LIMIT AT ENTRY
  applies, shadow and real.
- Free derived controls (no intents, no settlement, never seat, no charge): EVL-WL-O1-POOL and EVL-WL-ON-POOL, every
  kept candidate of each decision whose primary event is knowable after the version's registration, valued on its pool
  outcome (PREREG-EVL-7 draft section 6's construction).

 COMMON (both):  1 primary pool dummies (O1: 3, ON: 5; the first pool program the baseline); 2 multi (>= 2 pool programs
 select the issuer); 3 the items of the issuer's 8-Ks (any 8-K) in the decision's EDGAR window (O1: the open window
 of D; ON: the close window of D): 1.01, 2.01, 5.02, 7.01, 8.01, 9.01 (6);
 4 src_benzinga (primary article); hl = ln(1 + headline characters) / 5 (primary article; 0 otherwise); 5 n_art =
 ln(1 + single-symbol articles of the ticker knowable in the primary family's window), n_att20 (the same over the 20
 sessions before that window), first20 (the issuer in none of this learner's candidate sets of the previous 20
 sessions) (3); 6 mon, fri (2); 7 issuer from D's universe row and cik_info: ln(adv20/1e7), ln(prev_close/20),
 ln(sig20/0.03), bench_iwm, SIC bio/tech/fin/energy (8, PREREG-EVL-7 draft item 10's definitions); 8 r1, r5, r20 and
 hist_missing (PREREG-EVL-7 draft items 11-12) (4); 9 the 16 KEYWORDS flags and dup72 of evl7/reader/features_spec.py
 (copied with its sha256) (17); 10 FROM SEC HISTORY: ern_sched (the issuer has an item-2.02 8-K in that window and its
 previous item-2.02 8-K was accepted 70-110 days before it), ern_first (an item-2.02 8-K in that window and none in the

 EVL-WL-O1 ADDS: overnight [20:00, 04:00), pre [04:00, 07:00), late [07:00, 09:00) ET of the primary's knowable time;
 ln_ins = ln(1 + purchase value / $50,000) of an EVL-INS-BUY-O1 primary (4).
 EVL-WL-ON ADDS: morning (knowable 09:00-12:00 ET of D), before (knowable before 09:00 ET of D); dmove = clip(ln(m1530 /
 prev_close), +-0.5), dmove_missing, |dmove|; gap = clip(ln(m0930 / prev_close), +-0.5), gap_missing (the '1530' and

 So D = 54 columns for EVL-WL-O1 and 59 for EVL-WL-ON, g included.

W3. LABELS. y = clip(G / sig20, -5, 5), G the GROSS abnormal return of the candidate's one-session hold at the learner's
auctions: FORWARD (House): exit_px / entry_px - bench_exit / bench_entry of its POOL OUTCOME (outcomes status

W5. THE TRAINER (blake-desktop, deterministic; numpy allowed outside league/; reference environment pinned in the
receipt: x86-64, Python 3.14, numpy 2.5.3).
- GBT G (the nonlinear prior): target y~ = y minus its session mean; squared loss; every continuous column cut at its
  32 table quantiles (np.quantile, method 'linear', on the full table; binaries 2 bins); depth 3; learning rate 0.05;
  leaf value sum(residual) / (n + 2,000); a split needs >= 2,000 rows each side; gain (sum r_L)^2/(n_L + 2,000) +
  (sum r_R)^2/(n_R + 2,000) - (sum r)^2/(n + 2,000); ties to the lowest column, then the lowest bin; gradient sums by
  np.bincount in table order. ROUNDS R* (the one inner procedure, never read): fit on the first 80% of sessions, score
  the last 20% at 25, 50, 100, 150, 200 and 300 rounds by the mean within-session Spearman correlation of prediction and
  y; R* = the best, ties to fewer.
- CROSS-FIT: five contiguous session folds; fold k's g_oof from a G of R* rounds fitted on the other four; G_full of R*
  rounds on all rows (the House's g); g standardised by the mean and sd of g_oof over the table.
- RIDGE PRIOR: columns (W2 with g = g_oof); within-session demeaned; A_h = sum x~ x~', b_h = sum x~ y~ (products in
  float64, every entry summed by math.fsum: exact, order-free), N_h rows. PRIOR: A_0 = (N_EQ / N_h) A_h, b_0 = (N_EQ /
  N_h) b_h, N_0 = N_EQ with N_EQ = 25,000 rows (W12's calibration: the most robust measured weight). The prior never
  decays; the forward stream's steady weight at H = 40 is 58.2 x its rows a session (19,800-49,500 at the pools' 340-850
  candidates), so after about 40-75 sessions history and the live stream weigh about the same.
- OUTPUT: one canonical JSON model (column names, bin edges, trees, R*, g's mean and sd, A_0, b_0, N_0, TABLE_SHA, the
  code sha, and the outcome-free META: every 8-K acceptance time and its item-2.02 flag per CIK from 2025-08-01 to the
  snapshot, which item 10 unions with the House's own filings table) and its sha256 MODEL_SHA. THE TRAINER RUNS TWICE in one job: equal MODEL_SHA -> the model; unequal only in
  G -> the pre-registered FALLBACK (g := 0, its row and column dropped from A_0, b_0), that model's sha the MODEL_SHA;
  unequal in the ridge part -> no model (W7 VOID clock keeps running).
- RECEIPT (committed to docs/prereg/evl-9/TRAINING-RECEIPT.json within 1 hour of the job's end, by the job): TABLE_SHA,
  MODEL_SHA, the raw-input manifest's sha256, row counts by year and primary pool program, dropped rows by cause,
  fallback yes/no, start/end times, environment. NO fit statistic, coefficient, R*, score quantile or IC. A committed
  receipt binds: its model is the version's model whatever anyone later sees.
- The model and the table are MACHINE-ONLY: the model reaches the House by `floor_box.py put-data` (one upload, the
  House refuses a file whose sha256 is not MODEL_SHA); neither is committed, published or shown to any designer; R3's
  facts authorizer refuses them and every warm table.

W6. FORWARD UPDATE AND PICKS (the House, stdlib).
- NIGHTLY, after the score: for each entry session s whose every pool outcome is terminal, warm_stats(program, version,
  s, A_s, b_s, n_s) is written ONCE from the candidate set and features stored at tau_s (never recomputed) and W3's
  forward labels (math.fsum, cik order); a pool outcome settling later is never added.
- AT tau of D: A = A_0 + sum_s w_s A_s and b likewise over the warm_stats rows written before tau with n(D) - n(s) <= 250,
  w_s = 2^(-(n(D) - n(s)) / H), H = 40 sessions; N_w = N_0 + sum w_s n_s; d_j = sqrt(A_jj / N_w) (d_j < 1e-9: d_j := 1, its
  row and column 0); beta = diag(d)^-1 (diag(d)^-1 A diag(d)^-1 + 8,000 I)^-1 diag(d)^-1 b, Cholesky in float64.
- PREDICTION: e_i = (x_i - xbar_D) . beta (xbar_D over D's kept candidates); c_i = the replay's round trip for i
  (score.cost_bp entry + exit at adv20 and E_prev) / 1e4; z_i = e_i - c_i / sig20_i; ahat_i = sig20_i x z_i.
- PICKS: the eligible candidates with z_i > 0, ranked by ahat_i, then adv20 (higher first), then event id; the first
  K = 8 are the learner's intents of D (rank_key = ahat_i). No other pick exists; a decision may pick fewer or none.
- The lane's sessions before REGISTERED_AT_9 (from Oct 19) have their candidate sets RECONSTRUCTED ONCE as of each
  tau_s and their warm_stats written: they train, they never count.

W7. WARM-UP, FAILURE, THE RECORD BEFORE THE MODEL, VOID.
- No warm-up beyond the model: the first decision with a verified model picks.
- FAIL CLOSED: no verified model, an unreadable pool, outcome or feature, or a non-finite fit -> no pick; the receipt
  says why.
- ISOLATION: the warm part runs after every row of PREREG-EVL-1 to -8 at its auction, under its own bound (90 s at the
  open, 120 s at the close); its failure never cuts an earlier row.
- THE RECORD BEFORE THE MODEL AND THE RELEASE: every decision from REGISTERED_AT_9 to the first live one is decided BY
  ITSELF, once, oldest first, exactly as of its tau (every cut-off from stored times; the model's content is fixed by
  public records of sessions before 2026-10-16, current SIC excepted, and by nothing stored after tau), in shadow only, never traded, receipts
  `decide-open-wl:<day>` / `decide-close-wl:<day>`. Real money starts at the first decision after the release carrying
  the code is live AND the model is verified.
- VOID: if no training receipt is committed by the close of the 10th session after REGISTERED_AT_9, the version is
  VOID: it never seats, its record is never judged, and its charge is returned (it never had a chance to pass). A VOID
  is decided by the clock alone; once a receipt is committed the version is judged with its model, whatever follows.
