## Public data

The website reads saved JSON. Public visitors cannot start model requests.

- [Shared-context measurements](../public/shared-research-context.json),
  [prompt-selection decision](../public/self-improvement.json), and
  [current budget accounting](../public/research-budget.json) record the newest
  experiments without private prompts or model drafts.

- [Research universe](../public/universe.json) lists nine company profiles and
  official sources. A profile is not a reviewed investment case or a holding.
- [Thesis history](../public/portfolio.json) and [reviewed investigations](../public/investigations.json)
  contain selected published research. Their cost fields distinguish known estimates
  from unknown usage; they are not a reconciled provider bill.
- [Cash-flow bridge](../public/cashflow-bridge.json) contains independently checked
  issuer figures and source metadata.
- [Original dossier](../public/research-dossier.json) and
  [separate follow-up](../public/dossier-followup.json) contain measured checks,
  not approval of their model drafts.
- [Evaluation](../public/research-evaluation.json), [replay](../public/trajectory-replay.json),
  [cache pilot](../public/policy-experiment.json), and [robustness](../public/robustness.json)
  preserve experiment measurements. The [rejected scheduling configuration](../public/experiments/rejected-asap/policy-experiment.json)
  is retained separately.

Raw responses, private run databases, full source captures, and credentials are
not included in a public clone. Historical documents describe their recorded
protocols; consult the relevant current runner before starting new paid work.
