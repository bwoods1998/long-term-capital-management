# Release E4 in the swarm: the panel lane (package G)

The panel lane breeds programs that pick 5 to 12 names out of the day's S&P 500 members by a cross-sectional rule.
A panel program sees salted slots (no ticker, no date, no price level) and is judged on its daily RESIDUAL against
matched placebo portfolios: on Train by the bar equity-xs-v1, then by ONE Validation try and ONE sealed look under the
screen XS1. This file is the swarm's side of it: `league/swarm/elane_xs.py`, the job `league/ops/xs_zero_edge.py`,
and the one-line dispatches E1's and E2's modules gain (`league/swarm/e4_patches/G.md`). The engine, the store, the
screen and the style rule are `league/xs/` (packages C, D and E); the contract between the packages is
`league/xs/E4_INTERFACES.md`.

With `elane.panel.mode` "off" (as policy.json ships it) nothing here runs, spends or trades.

## 1. The switch

`elane.panel.mode` is a setting; THE EFFECTIVE MODE is `elane_xs.mode(settings, root)`, never higher than the setting:

| effective mode | what runs | what it needs beside the setting |
|---|---|---|
| "off" | nothing | (also read when `elane.mode` is not "gate", when the panel manifest is missing or cannot be read, or when its coverage verdict is "insufficient") |
| "train" | panel births, the Train bar, the review, the zero-edge job | a panel manifest whose verdict is "ok" or "low_coverage" |
| "gate" | Validation and XS1 looks | the pinned XS1 receipt (`elane.screens.equity_xs` of the REPOSITORY's policy.json: no receipt, no look) and `hs_share` at or above `spreads_min_share` (below it a Train pass is under provisional costs and takes no try) |
| "live" | a passer may take a seat | `live_ready`: the universe deploy in force (`live_from`), the tradable share, the universe's age, the last nightly I6, and the twin marker (`xs-twins.json`, written by the forward record's panel night, package J: with no marker, or one behind, "live" reads as "gate") |

Each step is a settings change by the captain, and each falls back to the one before on its own when a leg fails.
ROLLBACK: set the mode down one step. Under "gate" a panel family holds no seat, whatever its band: a Candidate is
seatless, and a family ALREADY at Probe has no row for the book either (`bands.read`), so its planned entries are held
at the send and, on the lane's own word (`seat_word`: "demoted" ONLY when the setting is under "live" or `elane.mode`
is off), the book retires its instance and its lots exit at the next open by E1's rule. A STORE-SIDE LEG that fails
with the switch left at "live" (the coverage verdict, the spreads' share, the receipt, any leg of `live_ready`) is
"held": its opens stop, its exits go on, and nothing is retired or sold on it (E4-SPEC 17); a lane that cannot be read
is "unknown", on which nothing is retired either. Under "train" no Validation and no look runs; under "off" nothing
does.

A CODE ROLLBACK past E4 (to a release from before it, such as main 8ab31e32) once the lane has been at "train" takes
one step first: set the mode "off", then retire every alive panel family (`scripts/panel_rollback.py --state
<state>` lists them; `--apply` retires each by `SwarmStore.retire`, and refuses while the setting is not "off" or a
panel family is outside the Gym band). A release from before E4 has no panel structure: its `OPTIONS_ONLY_SQL` is
"structure NOT IN ('etf', 'stock')", so its options readers read every alive panel family as an options family

counted the panel families). No money row moves (a Gym family holds no seat); the options lane's room would.
docs/operations.md, "Rollback past E4".

## 3. The round

BIRTHS (E2's equity pass, patch 6). The request gains the PANEL block (`elane_xs.request_text`) and the panel Train
map after E2's own; a proposal whose structure is "panel" is admitted by `elane_xs.admit` (its card's box, its cell
not closed, no same idea in research in its cell, a lineage that has taken no try, E2's quota by structure).

THE TRAIN BAR equity-xs-v1 (`elane_xs.score`; in-sample; never evidence). One run a SEEN SPAN, each starting flat:
the full universe at 1.0x (with its style block) and at 1.5x (with the Train placebos and its call facts), and each
asset half at 1.5x. Four runs a span, eight or twelve a version. The runs are read pooled.

| rule | on the seen spans' runs pooled | judges |
|---|---|---|
| XA0 | at least 40 rebalances with a non-empty target and a lot held on at least half the sessions (1.0x) | the program (refutes nothing; an inactive program is judged on no other rule) |
| XT1 | the residual's t at 1.5x at least 2.0 | the mechanism |
| XY1 | the residual's sum at 1.5x above zero in at least two thirds of the seen years (4 of 5; 4 of 6 with no hidden year) and in a year of each segment that holds a seen year | the mechanism |
| XH1 | that sum above zero in BOTH asset halves, run apart | the mechanism |
| XP1 | at most 5% of the matched placebos' pooled t at or above the program's (10 of 200) | the mechanism |
| XR1 | the sum at 1.0x above zero and the sum at 1.5x at least half of it | the mechanism |
| STATE | asked again after fresh loads (after calls 3, 10, 13, 20, ...), its targets match the uninterrupted run's on 99% of calls | the program |
| WINDOW | a result with a run day outside its span, or of 2016, 2020 or 2021: refused whole, an alert | the House |

THE LOOSENING, stated: six panel Train years, five of them seen (E2's bar reads seven and six). A family is REFUTED
only with a version judged on XT1, XY1, XH1, XP1 or XR1; else it is STALLED. The House's failures (a run the child
failed, a restart check that could not finish, a store being rewritten) score nothing and back off, as E2's do.

THE HOUSE'S CLOCK (`elane_xs.steady`; the ship check's fix). A decide's limit is one second of WALL time
(league/gym/runtime.py; league/xs/program.py reads a wall clock beside it) and a research child runs at nice 19 on
one core: in the rehearsal on the House's box a program that needs a tenth of a second a decide lost decides to the
limit and gave different lots on the same span run to run. So NO RUN WHOSE RUNTIME SHOWS A DECIDE TIMEOUT IS JUDGED
OR RECORDED: it is made again, `CLOCK_RUNS` (3) times at most, and the first run with none is the one read (the
bar's runs, the hidden run, the zero-edge job's and the forward night's alike). When every run of the bound shows
one:
- THE PROGRAM'S, when they repeat as a program's do: the same count in every run, or more than `SLOW_SHARE` (5%)
  of its calls in every run. The version's verdict is `SLOW` (a program fault like RUN, DQ and LOAD: it judges the
  program, never the mechanism, refutes nothing and spends one of the family's versions), and its words carry what
  was measured: how many calls of how many ran past the limit in each run, the seconds its decides took in all and
  a call on average. Its author reads them in the next brief.
- THE HOUSE'S, when they do not: nothing is scored, the version waits out a backoff (`house_failure`, kind
  "clock") and is judged when the box is steady; the third in a row is one alert (`elane_xs_clock`).
A decide timeout inside the restart check stops the check as the House's (the runs it reads showed none). THE FIX AT
THE ROOT is the child's own CPU time in league/gym/runtime.py, which is inside the equity identity: a release of its
own. VALIDATION AND THE LOOK ASK THE RULE TOO (step two; `steady_runs`): each run of the Validation try (2025 at k
1.0 and 1.5), the two Train segments both read and the look's sealed run (the same seed and draws in every run of the
bound) is made through `steady`, `may_run` asked before EACH run, so no second run starts inside a session wall. The
House's clock spends nothing at first: the try or the look is owed after a backoff (`gate_clock_fail`, the swarm
store's kv `elane_xs_gate_clock`), the third in a row one `elane_xs_clock` alert. Timeouts that repeat as a program's
are SLOW: the try is spent (a "slow" Validation run, no pre-check), the look spent and FAILED. (A slow Train-segment
run at Validation or at the look is read as the House's clock: the bar's runs of those years were steady.) THE OWE
HAS AN END (`GATE_CLOCK_SPENDS`; the review of step two): a program sees its own equity in a run, so on a losing path
of a sealed window it could make its decides straddle the limit and have its sealed read never count. A round of the
House's clock in which a SEALED run was made is counted (the row's `sealed`): the Validation try is SPENT at the third
such round (a "clock" run, no pre-check, an alert that says so), and at the look each such round is a send of the
sealed run beside a run that never returned (E1's `LOOK_SENDS`, one count in `elane_look_lost`), so the second spends
and FAILS the look. While a version owes its try's sealed run its verdict is marked owed (`OWED`: `elane.next_try`
asks it first) and NO OTHER VERSION OF ITS LINEAGE TAKES THE TRY (`try_owed`: they wait; once the owing family is
retired the lineage's try is spent). A round at the Train segments reads nothing sealed and is owed with no end, as
before. (One try a lineage makes one look a lineage: a second version is never gate-ready while one owes its look.)
THE COST, STATED AS E1 STATES ITS OWN: a box whose clock is genuinely unsteady can spend a good program's try or
look. FOR FWD1-R's CASE N (A33, build B11): a lineage reached a sealed pre-check once any version made the try's
sealed run, whether it was read, spent or is owed (a recorded try, a verdict marked `OWED`, a clock row with
`sealed`), and once any look's sealed run was sent (`elane_look_lost`, `look_inflight`).

THE MEMORY OF A JUDGMENT (`elane_xs.one_store`, `bar_runs`, `slicer`; the ship check's fix). The panel runner opens
the store for every run and each open maps the two bar arrays again; a slice of three Train years is about 45 MB at
the real panel's size and the parent holds it three times over while it is sent. A judgment reads ONE store, keeps
each run's result and lets its slice go, and the restart check cuts a k 1.5 run's slice again when it reads it, one
span's at a time (the stream refuses a slice that is not the run's: every rebuilt ctx is held to the sha the run
recorded). Kept whole, the ten runs of a judgment were 762 MB in the swarm's process on the box and more than the
ops child's 500 MB in the zero-edge job.
After the verdict is recorded the HIDDEN RUN is made, into `elane_xs_oof` and nowhere else. The style reading
(`league.xs.style`) is kept in the version's record whatever the bar said. Every run asks `league.xs.config.quiet`
with the run budget first, then the day's CPU. A PANEL RUN'S WALL IS THE WHOLE SESSION (`quiet(..., session=True)`,
`league.xs.config.RUN_SESSION_ET`: the box has one core): on a session a run starts only when it would be done
before 09:25 New York time, or after 16:35 (13:35 on a half day); a weekend and a holiday have no wall. While no
run may start a panel family takes no step, a model question included (`blocked`): the lane researches at night and
on weekends. Births are not held.

THE REVIEW (`elane_xs.review_step`). E2's review machinery under the panel rubric (`elane_xs.REVIEW_XS`: no embedded
return table, no calendar recognition, no use of the slot ids beyond naming targets, no constant that names a
calendar period, no search for a price level; and lookahead). The record E1's look reads names the rubric
(`REVIEW_OBJECTIVE`); a look takes a review under no other.

VALIDATION (`elane_xs.validate`; mode "gate"). In this order: both Train series (the residual x and the universe
excess u, by session, from the two whole Train segments at 1.0x) are KEPT; the LINEAGE is asked (the same template,
or 0.7 or more in x OR in u against a panel program already looked at or refused at its pre-check); then the 2025
run at 1.0x and 1.5x and the screen's pre-check. One try a lineage (E1's ration); no Validation-only retry. A program
the lineage rule refuses takes no try and its family retires for it.

THE LOOK (`elane_xs.look`). It WAITS (nothing spent) on: no pinned receipt; THE A31 LOOK HOLD (`look_rule` "hold",
the policy's default, or any value this tree does not implement: "A31: no XS1 look before XS1-R rules", with every
other leg in place; Validation is not held); its agenda cell's two looks taken (A22);
no review under the panel rubric; no published contamination probe with the panel strata for the route that wrote
it (A22: read from THE LOOK PERMIT, `<state>/xs-look-permit.json`, two words a stratum and era, which the probe's job
writes; never from the probe's own file, which release E3's seal lets no module of league/swarm name); a panel store
that ends before the calibrated holdout; a stale membership; the quiet rule. It is REFUSED
(no look spent, the family retires for the stage) on: the lineage's look spent, an ineligible program floor, the
same lineage, a recorded Validation that no longer passes. Then ONE sealed run (Validation's first session through
the holdout, the receipt's placebo draws under the seed fixed from the receipt's and the code's sha256), judged by
`league.xs.screen.verdict`; the receipt's sentence, with the House's count of earlier looks in the cell, is recorded
with the look and stated beside every live trade. A sealed run sent and never answered is owed once, then spent and
failed; one its own errors ended is spent and failed, never judged. THE BAR is the pinned receipt's and is read at
the look, never written here: since the captain's A23 the receipt in the tree is the study's rebuilt at its own
bias-raised threshold, above the registered one (docs/operations.md, **The screen XS1**: what moved, why, the cost).

THE SEAT. A pass makes a Candidate (band proof {"engine", "xs_sha256", "run_sha"}) ONLY when the style rule reads
the program as not style-open. A style-open passer (or one with no style reading, or whose sentence cannot be
filled) is RECORDED AND SHADOWED, NEVER SEATED: no band, no proof, alive in the Gym band; the forward route is its
way to a seat. A Candidate holds a seat only while the effective mode is "live" (`elane_xs.seatable`).

## 4. The zero-edge column

`league/ops/xs_zero_edge.py` runs, each week, `zero_edge_programs` random-score programs through the whole bar (the
restart check included) on its own niced decider, 25 a run, and keeps {week: {programs, passes, by_rule}} in the
swarm store's kv. The Train map shows each cell's passes beside births x that pass rate. A program's picks are a
hash of its seed, the slot id and a key read from the market's own path in ctx; it keeps nothing between calls (a
call counter would fail the bar's restart check every time). A chunk stops itself before its own wall (no further
program once the time it has worked plus its slowest program's would pass its working budget) and the next trigger
resumes the week; a program the House could not judge is not counted (a run the child failed, a restart check that
could not finish, decide timeouts in every run of the clock rule's bound: a random-score program has no slow decide
of its own). An alarm when a whole week's rate is above 5%. ON THE BOX THAT ALARM CANNOT FIRE YET: a Sunday's four
chunks finish about 32 to 40 programs (docs/operations.md, "On the box's one core"), so at `zero_edge_programs` 100
no week is ever whole; a rolling rate over the kept weeks, or the setting at what a Sunday fits, is owed before
"gate" (step two). A chunk reads one panel store and a judgment keeps no
slice (section 3, THE MEMORY OF A JUDGMENT): `league/tests/test_xs_zero_edge.py` `TheJobFitsItsMemory` runs three
judgments at the real panel's size under the ops child's own 500 MB and holds 30 MB of headroom (about 80 measured
on the desk with the engine canned; 57 in the real job run whole there; not measured on the box).

## 7. The merge with main's releases E5-A and E3.1 (Oct 11, 2026): what three new rules do for a panel program

The lane is merged with main `8ab31e32`, the release the six seated ETF programs trade their first session under
(deployed 22:51Z Oct 11, 2026). That main brought three rules the panel kind had never met. Each is decided here and
held by `league/tests/test_equity_panel_on_main.py`. What a share program does under them is main's, order for order
(`league/tests/test_equity_etf_orders.py`, recorded on that main).

**1. The seat rule ("activity-1-free"; the captain's A24 with A30). IN STEP ONE A PANEL FAMILY NEVER ENTERS THE SEAT
PLAN.** It never counts against the six seats, never holds a seat and never yields one. IN STEP TWO A PANEL FAMILY
HAS A POOL OF ITS OWN (`equity_money.panel.seats`, 1; the swarm's `elane.panel.seats` may only lower it): the book
runs the rule a pool at a time over the rows of each pool's kind (`league/equity/live.py` `_pools`), so the six ETF
seats, their plan, the idle test and K are main's whatever the panel families' bands, and a panel family never
takes, holds or yields one of them. The ETF pool's plan is `eq_seat` as before; the panel pool's is its key
"panel". A panel program held on a failed leg (no row: its opens stop) keeps its seat in the pool, and nobody is
seated into it before or after the leg recovers (`_held_seats`). The swarm's reader seats by pool too
(league/swarm/bands.py `equity_seating`), so under the rollback "first-come" a panel Probe family costs no ETF
