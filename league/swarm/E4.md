# Release E4 in the swarm: the panel lane (package G)

The panel lane breeds programs that pick 5 to 12 names out of the day's S&P 500 members by a cross-sectional rule.
A panel program sees salted slots (no ticker, no date, no price level) and is judged on its daily RESIDUAL against
matched placebo portfolios: on Train by the bar equity-xs-v1, then by ONE Validation try and ONE sealed look under the
screen XS1. This file is the swarm's side of it: `league/swarm/elane_xs.py`, the job `league/ops/xs_zero_edge.py`,
and the one-line dispatches E1's and E2's modules gain (`league/swarm/e4_patches/G.md`). The engine, the store, the
screen and the style rule are `league/xs/` (packages C, D and E); the contract between the packages is
`league/xs/E4_INTERFACES.md`.

With `elane.panel.mode` "off" (as policy.json ships it) nothing here runs, spends or trades.

## 1. The switch

`elane.panel.mode` is a setting; THE EFFECTIVE MODE is `elane_xs.mode(settings, root)`, never higher than the setting:

| effective mode | what runs | what it needs beside the setting |
|---|---|---|
| "off" | nothing | (also read when `elane.mode` is not "gate", when the panel manifest is missing or cannot be read, or when its coverage verdict is "insufficient") |
| "train" | panel births, the Train bar, the review, the zero-edge job | a panel manifest whose verdict is "ok" or "low_coverage" |
| "gate" | Validation and XS1 looks | the pinned XS1 receipt (`elane.screens.equity_xs` of the REPOSITORY's policy.json: no receipt, no look) and `hs_share` at or above `spreads_min_share` (below it a Train pass is under provisional costs and takes no try) |
| "live" | a passer may take a seat | `live_ready`: the universe deploy in force (`live_from`), the tradable share, the universe's age, the last nightly I6, and the twin marker (package J's `xs-twins.json`: with J unmerged, "live" reads as "gate") |

Each step is a settings change by the captain, and each falls back to the one before on its own when a leg fails.
ROLLBACK: set the mode down one step. Under "gate" a panel Candidate is seatless (no row for the book, so no new
entry; its lots exit by E1's rules); under "train" no Validation and no look runs; under "off" nothing does.

Settings (`elane.panel`; a value outside its bound reads as its default; swarm.json over policy.json):

| key | default | bound | what |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mode` | "off" | off, train, gate, live | the ladder above |
| `panels` | ["sp500"] | inside `league.xs.PANELS` | |
| `birth_share` | 0.5 | [0, 1] | the panel's share of the equity lanes' births, held over E2's birth window |
| `max_alive` | 12 | [0, 100] | panel families in Train research |
| `max_names` | 12 | [5, 40] | a card's ceiling |
| `rebalance` | ["weekly", "monthly"] | inside `league.xs.config.REBALANCE` | |
| `train_budget` | 4 | [1, 4] | scored versions a family |
| `placebos_train` | 200 | [200, 1000] | the Train bar's placebos (XP1) |
| `cpu_seconds_day` | 14400 | [0, 36000] | child seconds of panel runs a UTC day; spent: the round waits |
| `run_budget_seconds` | 600 | [60, 600] | one run's budget, and what the quiet rule is asked with before each run |
| `zero_edge_programs` | 100 | [20, 400] | random-score programs a week |
| `coverage_min` | 0.97 | [0.97, 1.0] | the data job's (tighten only) |
| `spreads_min_share` | 0.90 | [0.5, 1.0] | below it the mode is at most "train" |
| `membership_max_stale_days` | 45 | [7, 120] | no look past it |
| `universe_max_age_days` | 400 | [30, 500] | `live_ready` |
| `live_min_tradable_share` | 0.95 | [0.90, 1.0] | `live_ready` |
| `census_notes` | {} | {agenda: text of at most 200 characters} | swarm.json only; shown under "in-sample 2010-24, best of 7; not evidence" |

The researcher's pace, attempts, error counts, tokens, review routes and day's dollars are E2's own (`elane.e2`).

## 2. A panel family

Lane "equity", structure "panel", `roots` [] (it names no symbol), spec {`lane`, `agenda`, `panel`, `max_names`,
`rebalance`, `weekday`, `e2_route`, `hidden_year`, `gid`}. Its agenda CELL is (lane, agenda, "panel:" + panel)
(`elane_xs.cell_of`); the agenda is `elane_xs.PANEL_AGENDA`. A cell with E2's `refuted_cell_cap` families refuted on a
judged rule and no pass is CLOSED.

- THE TRAIN RECORD: family state `elane_xs`, E2's record's shape ({status, versions, passed_version, review,
  passed_review, attempts, refused, infra}), read by a generator through `elane_xs.train_facts` only.
- SEALED (never in a request, a brief, a map or a route choice): `elane_xs_floor`, `elane_xs_train_x`,
  `elane_xs_train_u`, `elane_xs_precheck`, `elane_xs_placebo`, `elane_xs_halves`, `elane_xs_oof`, `elane_xs_look`,
  beside E1's own (`elane_try`, `elane_validation`, `elane_verdicts`, `validation_r_sd_by_version`, the band).
- THE HIDDEN YEAR: `elane_xs.assign` draws one of the panel's six Train years by cell while release E3's folds are
  recording, else none. No fact a researcher reads names a calendar year (the seen years are counted in time order).
- kv: `elane_xs_cpu` {utc day: seconds}, `elane_xs_zero_edge` {week: {programs, passes, by_rule}},
  `elane_xs_train_ok_at`.

## 3. The round

BIRTHS (E2's equity pass, patch 6). The request gains the PANEL block (`elane_xs.request_text`) and the panel Train
map after E2's own; a proposal whose structure is "panel" is admitted by `elane_xs.admit` (its card's box, its cell
not closed, no same idea in research in its cell, a lineage that has taken no try, E2's quota by structure).

THE TRAIN BAR equity-xs-v1 (`elane_xs.score`; in-sample; never evidence). One run a SEEN SPAN, each starting flat:
the full universe at 1.0x (with its style block) and at 1.5x (with the Train placebos and its call facts), and each
asset half at 1.5x. Four runs a span, eight or twelve a version. The runs are read pooled.

| rule | on the seen spans' runs pooled | judges |
|---|---|---|
| XA0 | at least 40 rebalances with a non-empty target and a lot held on at least half the sessions (1.0x) | the program (refutes nothing; an inactive program is judged on no other rule) |
| XT1 | the residual's t at 1.5x at least 2.0 | the mechanism |
| XY1 | the residual's sum at 1.5x above zero in at least two thirds of the seen years (4 of 5; 4 of 6 with no hidden year) and in a year of each segment that holds a seen year | the mechanism |
| XH1 | that sum above zero in BOTH asset halves, run apart | the mechanism |
| XP1 | at most 5% of the matched placebos' pooled t at or above the program's (10 of 200) | the mechanism |
| XR1 | the sum at 1.0x above zero and the sum at 1.5x at least half of it | the mechanism |
| STATE | asked again after fresh loads (after calls 3, 10, 13, 20, ...), its targets match the uninterrupted run's on 99% of calls | the program |
| WINDOW | a result with a run day outside its span, or of 2016, 2020 or 2021: refused whole, an alert | the House |

THE LOOSENING, stated: six panel Train years, five of them seen (E2's bar reads seven and six). A family is REFUTED
only with a version judged on XT1, XY1, XH1, XP1 or XR1; else it is STALLED. The House's failures (a run the child
failed, a restart check that could not finish, a store being rewritten) score nothing and back off, as E2's do.
After the verdict is recorded the HIDDEN RUN is made, into `elane_xs_oof` and nowhere else. The style reading
(`league.xs.style`) is kept in the version's record whatever the bar said. Every run asks `league.xs.config.quiet`
with the run budget first, then the day's CPU.

THE REVIEW (`elane_xs.review_step`). E2's review machinery under the panel rubric (`elane_xs.REVIEW_XS`: no embedded
return table, no calendar recognition, no use of the slot ids beyond naming targets, no constant that names a
calendar period, no search for a price level; and lookahead). The record E1's look reads names the rubric
(`REVIEW_OBJECTIVE`); a look takes a review under no other.

VALIDATION (`elane_xs.validate`; mode "gate"). In this order: both Train series (the residual x and the universe
excess u, by session, from the two whole Train segments at 1.0x) are KEPT; the LINEAGE is asked (the same template,
or 0.7 or more in x OR in u against a panel program already looked at or refused at its pre-check); then the 2025
run at 1.0x and 1.5x and the screen's pre-check. One try a lineage (E1's ration); no Validation-only retry. A program
the lineage rule refuses takes no try and its family retires for it.

THE LOOK (`elane_xs.look`). It WAITS (nothing spent) on: no pinned receipt; its agenda cell's two looks taken (A22);
no review under the panel rubric; no published contamination probe with the panel strata for the route that wrote
it (A22); a panel store that ends before the calibrated holdout; a stale membership; the quiet rule. It is REFUSED
(no look spent, the family retires for the stage) on: the lineage's look spent, an ineligible program floor, the
same lineage, a recorded Validation that no longer passes. Then ONE sealed run (Validation's first session through
the holdout, the receipt's placebo draws under the seed fixed from the receipt's and the code's sha256), judged by
`league.xs.screen.verdict`; the receipt's sentence, with the House's count of earlier looks in the cell, is recorded
with the look and stated beside every live trade. A sealed run sent and never answered is owed once, then spent and
failed; one its own errors ended is spent and failed, never judged.

THE SEAT. A pass makes a Candidate (band proof {"engine", "xs_sha256", "run_sha"}) ONLY when the style rule reads
the program as not style-open. A style-open passer (or one with no style reading, or whose sentence cannot be
filled) is RECORDED AND SHADOWED, NEVER SEATED: no band, no proof, alive in the Gym band; the forward route is its
way to a seat. A Candidate holds a seat only while the effective mode is "live" (`elane_xs.seatable`).

## 4. The zero-edge column

`league/ops/xs_zero_edge.py` runs, each week, `zero_edge_programs` random-score programs through the whole bar (the
restart check included) on its own niced decider, 25 a run, and keeps {week: {programs, passes, by_rule}} in the
swarm store's kv. The Train map shows each cell's passes beside births x that pass rate. A program's picks are a
hash of its seed, the slot id and a key read from the market's own path in ctx; it keeps nothing between calls (a
call counter would fail the bar's restart check every time). An alarm when a whole week's rate is above 5%.

## 5. Events and alerts the lane writes

`swarm.elane` {"pass": "scored" | "refuted" | "stalled", "structure": "panel"}; `swarm.status` actions
`elane_validation`, `elane_validation_refused`, `elane_validation_error` (each with "structure": "panel"),
`elane_xs_window` (alert), `elane_xs_state` (alert), `equity_look_lost` (alert), `panel_style_open`,
`panel_unstated` (alert); `swarm.gate` actions `look` (screen "XS1"), `refused`, `equity_look_waits`, `look_failed`,
`look_lost`, `equity_validation_moved`, `panel_lineage_restored`, `panel_lineage_unread`. Verdicts, sentences and
the probe's reading travel in private keys (a leading underscore) only.

## 6. What this release does not hold yet

- Package J (the forward twin) and the probe's panel strata wait for release E3: until then no panel look runs and
  "live" reads as "gate".
- The seat of a style-open passer is the forward route's, which is specified and not built.
- The tests fake `league.xs` at its interfaces (`league/tests/elane_xs_fakes.py`), as E1's gate tests fake the
  runner and the screen; a test that holds one of E1's or E2's patched files is skipped until the patch is applied.
