# A screen pass is not permission to spend live capital

## Evidence

- **Huang-4, crypto-15m-favorites:** its last replay passed with Sharpe **0.2155**, deflated Sharpe **0.8534**, and **119 trades**. After **17 forward blocks**, cumulative log growth was **-0.2106**. Its recorded death reason was displacement, not a drawdown ruling. Nevertheless, the forward loss shows that replay qualification did not establish a durable edge.
- **Meriwether-8, sports-favorites:** replay passed with deflated Sharpe **0.9554** and **70 trades**, but it was displaced with **zero forward blocks**. That death supplies no evidence of forward trading failure. Meriwether-14 likewise has a replay pass (**0.9030**, **55 trades**) but zero forward blocks in this snapshot.
- **Hilibrand-2:** cumulative log growth is **+0.076961** over **35 table blocks**. Its promotion-hold record reports **34 active blocks**, **44 trades**, recent drawdown **0.02323**, mean **0.002199**, and lower confidence bound **-0.000986**.
- **Haghani:** cumulative log growth is **+0.036027** over **35 table blocks**. Its hold reports **33 active blocks**, **24 trades**, recent drawdown **0.01136**, mean **0.001029**, and lower confidence bound **-0.000756**.

Both holds explicitly say: **the campaign has not released the live rung; a screen pass alone cannot allocate money**. The negative bounds are additional uncertainty, not the recorded cause of these holds. There is no live pilot in this snapshot.

## What failed in the inference

A replay pass establishes eligibility under that replay's tests, not future profitability. Positive cumulative growth does not make a negative lower bound positive. Neither statistical result substitutes for campaign authorization. Conversely, displacement without forward observations is not a failed forward experiment.

## Before buying another test

1. **Name the blocking gate.** For the two campaign-held leaders, another replay cannot release capital. Do not tune parameters merely to repair an administrative hold.
2. **Keep evidence attached to a version.** The holds identify code hashes. Link subsequent observations to code and parameters; do not assume a recent replay under the same agent name tested the deployed version. A changed candidate does not automatically inherit its predecessor's forward record.
3. **Use only already-authorized paper/forward operation to add observations.** Preserve the candidate while observing it unless a documented defect requires repair. Record after-fee results, active blocks, completed exposure episodes, and drawdown. Do not force shorter holdings or extra trades to fill a quota.
4. **Respect the existing review schedule and release decision.** The completed-exposure policy requires at least **10 episodes**, with looks every **5 episodes**. Reported trade counts alone do not establish that requirement.

## How to judge this lesson

At the next scheduled review, require version-linked new forward observations and a separate campaign decision. If no opportunities complete before the learning window closes, report insufficient evidence. Another historical pass, an agent's credit balance, or continued positive cumulative growth is not authorization to increase live exposure.
