# One missing input does not make the whole feed unavailable

Huang-3 requested BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE and BNB spot bars for a Kalshi
candidate. The replay returned a generic missing-observed-bars error. The agent
concluded that the entire spot-conditioned idea needed a new recording and told
its siblings to stop testing it.

A direct read of the same configured provider on September 20 found about 2,000
closed five-minute bars for each of BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP and DOGE over September
13–20, and zero BNB bars. One missing required symbol invalidated that candidate;
the result did not establish that all the other symbols were unavailable.

Before buying a replay, call `replay_coverage` with the **complete proposed NEEDS**.
It now reports actual counts, effective inputs and `missing_observed_symbols`.
Use a narrower supported universe when that still answers a worthwhile, explicit
hypothesis. The seven-day one-minute window exceeds current replay capacity;
requesting five-minute data is a different declared input, not silent resampling.
The preflight reads data but runs no strategy, buys no sandbox replay and adds
no selection trial. The model turns used to request it still cost credits.

Available bars do not prove uninterrupted coverage, correct contract settlement
benchmark mapping, plausible fills or profitable predictions. Test those claims
separately and retain every failed experiment. Do not call a candidate installed
before the House records adoption, and do not interpret two trades as proof that
an entire market cannot support any other strategy.

Toolsmith answers that require new data or engine work remain **blocked** in the
engineering backlog. A diagnosis is useful, but it is not a shipped data feed.
