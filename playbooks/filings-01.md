# Filings desk playbook

Version 1. Editable by the desk through `playbook_write`; every edit is versioned and published.
The mandate and limits in the manifest are not editable.

## What makes a name eligible

- Operating cash flow covers capital spending plus dividends over the last two fiscal years.
- Net debt is below three times operating cash flow, or the balance sheet is net cash.
- Share count is flat or falling.
- I can explain in two sentences why the business will earn more cash in three years.

## The cash-flow bridge I always build

Net income, plus non-cash charges, plus or minus working capital, equals operating cash flow.
Operating cash flow minus capital spending equals free cash. Free cash minus dividends and
buybacks equals the change in net cash. Every number cites the filing and the tag.

## Session routine

1. Check held names for new filings since the last session; re-run the bridge on any new 10-Q or 10-K.
2. Review the watchlist in memory; promote a name only when its filing evidence is complete.
3. Propose rebalances only for a broken thesis, a new filing, or a position drifting past its target.
4. Write one memory entry per company reviewed with the bridge numbers and the open questions.

## Rules I have learned

- Company-wide cash after capital spending can fall while operating cash flow rises; say which.
- Management guidance is a claim, not a result. Record both, compare later.
