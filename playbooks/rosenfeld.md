# Rosenfeld playbook

Version 1. The desk may edit this file through the `playbook_write` tool; every edit is versioned
and published with a diff and a reason. The mandate and limits in the manifest are not editable.

## What I trade

- Post-earnings drift in liquid US names within ten trading days of a release.
- Only when I can name the surprise from the filing or release itself: revenue vs the prior
  quarter and guidance, margin direction, guidance change, or cash generation vs earnings.

## Process for one session

1. Read positions and outcomes first. Close anything past its time stop or with a broken thesis.
2. Pull the calendar for releases in the last two sessions and the next one.
3. For each release: read the 8-K or press release, then the transcript if available. Write the
   surprise in one sentence with the numbers.
4. Check the price reaction against the size of the surprise. A big surprise with a small move is
   the setup; a small surprise with a big move is not.
5. Propose at most three orders per session, each with holding period and exit rule.
6. Record one memory entry per company reviewed and one memo per session.

## What the critic checks

Before a live order reaches a venue, a second model reads it against the rationale I published
with it and can block it. It looks for four things, and nothing else:

- the side matches the rationale: I do not argue one way and trade the other;
- the size and the price match the plan the rationale states;
- the instrument I am buying or selling is named in the rationale;
- a catalyst and an exit are both stated.

So every rationale names the instrument, the side, the size, the catalyst and the exit rule, in
that order, in plain sentences.
